=====================================================================
M2 ADDENDUM — isse canonical prompt ke NEECHE paste karo
(canonical file: GENSPARK_M1_2_ONE_SHOT_SCENE_RESEARCH_PROMPT.txt)
=====================================================================

Ye addendum canonical prompt ke rules NAHI badalta. Sirf do cheezein add karta
hai jo M2 engine use karta hai. Baaki sab (anti-hallucination, exact locators,
enum values, script coverage) waisa hi rehta hai.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
A. COVERAGE AB SECONDS SE NAAPI JAATI HAI, MOMENT-COUNT SE NAHI
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Purani galti: 82 moments mein se 65% ke paas locator tha — sunne mein theek,
par un 19 "analysis" moments ne 242 SECONDS le liye. Yaani aadhi video bina
footage ke thi.

Isliye ab:

1. Har moment ka approximate narration duration socho (script ke shabd / ~2.6
   shabd per second). 15+ second ka ek moment mat banao — use 2-3 chhote beats
   mein todo.
2. Target: kam se kam 75% narration SECONDS ke paas verified EXACT_TIME ya
   DIALOGUE evidence ho (jahan public source maujood hai).
3. Sirf text/graphic wale moments milakar 10% seconds se zyada na hon.
4. Abstract/analysis lines ke liye bhi pehle koi CONTEXT scene dhoondo usi
   already-approved source se — seedha TEXT_CARD mat do.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
B. HAR MOMENT MEIN "fallback_plan" DO (naya, optional par bahut useful)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Engine ke paas ab keyframe bank hai: jo sources tum approve karte ho, unke
frames se hi still/montage banta hai (sahi show, sahi character — koi random
image nahi). Usse behtar chunne ke liye ye field do:

"fallback_plan": {
  "allowed_pack_ids": ["P01"],
  "allowed_source_ids": ["P01_S01", "P01_S02"],
  "frame_hints": [
    { "source_id": "P01_S01", "time_sec": 132.5, "reason": "Nicole beside the fridge chart" },
    { "source_id": "P01_S02", "time_sec": 47.0,  "reason": "Nicole exhausted at the table" }
  ],
  "must_show": ["Nicole Watterson"],
  "must_not_show": ["reaction host", "wrong show", "fan art"],
  "template": "QUOTE",
  "overlay_text": "Nothing was ever enough."
}

Rules:
- **frame_hints sabse important hai.** Engine mein koi semantic/AI image-search
  NAHI hai — wo deterministic hai. Isliye jis second par sahi visual hai, wo
  SECOND do (source_id + time_sec). Tumne source dekha hi hai, to ye mushkil nahi.
  Ek moment ke liye 1-3 hints achhe rehte hain.
- Hint na do to bhi chalega: engine us moment ke source-time ke aas-paas ka best
  frame khud le lega. Par hint se accuracy kaafi behtar hoti hai.
- allowed_source_ids sirf allowed_pack_ids ke andar ke sources ho sakte hain
  (validator isse enforce karta hai — cross-show bleed rok raha hai).
- overlay_text sirf script/research se verbatim aaye — kuch invent mat karo.
- template values: COMPARISON | QUOTE | TIMELINE | CHECKLIST | NONE
  (COMPARISON par engine 2-3 frames ka montage banata hai.)
- `keyframe_queries` optional hai aur base engine ise IGNORE karta hai (wo sirf
  future optional CLIP module ke liye hai). Uspar coverage mat chhodo — frame_hints do.

IMPORTANT — jin packs ke paas apne sources nahi hain (jaise analysis/graphics pack):
engine unhe USI SHOW ke doosre packs ke frames use karne deta hai, agar poore
project mein sirf ek hi show hai. Cross-show project mein aisa nahi hoga —
wahan tumhe `allowed_pack_ids` explicitly dena hoga, warna wo beat plain text
graphic ban jayega (jo report mein alag ginā jayega).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
C. HOOK / HARD EVIDENCE BEATS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

Jo beats sabse zaroori hain (video ka hook, ya jahan narration kehta hai "watch
this exact scene"), unke liye:

- "criticality": "HOOK" ya "HARD_EVIDENCE" field do;
- jahan possible ho DO real candidates do (primary + alternate), taaki ek fail
  ho to doosra chal jaye.

Baaki normal beats ke liye "criticality": "NORMAL" (ya field chhod do).
