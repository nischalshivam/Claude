KITNA BADHNA CHAHIYE?
---------------------
Iska koi FIX number nahi hai — aur kisi ko bhi aapse fix number nahi maangna
chahiye (maine pichhli baar "68 hona chahiye" likh diya tha, wo galat tha).

Wajah simple hai: ye merge ek UNION hai. Dono packs ka jod. Aapke abhi wale
pack mein bhi kuch aisa evidence ho sakta hai jo purane mein tha hi nahi.
Isliye nateeja 68 se zyada bhi ho sakta hai aur kam bhi — dono theek hain.

Sirf DO cheezein dekhni hain, aur tool aakhir mein khud ye line chhapta hai:

    [THEEK HAI] Kuch khoya nahi, aur N nayi validated cheezein aayi hain.

    1. KUCH KHOYA NAHI    -> koi number ghata nahi hona chahiye
    2. POSITIVE UNION     -> kuch na kuch naya aana chahiye

Agar "[RUKO] Kuch evidence GHAT gaya" dikhe to --apply mat chalana, mujhe
output\pack-merge-report.json bhej dena.


TEEN ALAG NUMBER HAIN — INHE MILAO MAT
--------------------------------------
    locators (EXACT_TIME/DIALOGUE)  ek scene ka pata: kis video mein, kis waqt
    frame hints (alag-alag frame)   ek locator ke andar kai exact frame ho sakte hain
    moments par locator/hint        kitne moments ke paas kuch hai (ye alag ginti hai)

Ek moment par 1 locator aur 4 frame hints ho sakte hain. Isliye "hints" ka
number hamesha "locators" se bada dikhta hai — ye galti nahi hai.


LAGANA HAI TO
-------------

KYA SURAKSHIT RAHEGA
--------------------
  - aapke theek kiye hue cue          -> waise ke waise
  - har moment ki criticality          -> waisi ki waisi
  - source health (WORKING/DEAD)       -> waisi ki waisi

Sirf wo locators aur frame hints aayenge jo abhi pack mein hain hi nahi, aur
har ek pehle check hoga (source maujood hai? timestamp source ki length ke
andar hai?).
