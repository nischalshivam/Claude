YE FOLDER KYA HAI
=================

Isme M3.6.1 wala REPAIRED pack ki copy hai (68 locators, 192 frame hints).

Ye JAAN-BOOJH kar input\ mein nahi rakha gaya.

Kyun? Aapke input\scene-research.json mein abhi jo pack hai wo zyada NAYA hai:
usme 31 cue theek ho chuke hain aur 109 moments par criticality likhi ja chuki
hai. Us file ko replace karna aapka kaam mita dega.

Aur ye reference pack usme se wo cheezein rakhta hai jo folder replace karte
waqt kho gayi thi: 19 locators aur 19 frame hints.

DONO CHAHIYE. Isliye replace mat karo - MERGE karo:

    node tools\merge-pack.js input\scene-research.json reference-pack\M3.6.1-repaired-scene-research.json

    ^ ye sirf DIKHAYEGA ki kya-kya wapas aayega. Pack abhi nahi badlega.
      Theek lage to yahi command dobara, aage --apply laga kar:

    node tools\merge-pack.js input\scene-research.json reference-pack\M3.6.1-repaired-scene-research.json --apply

Isse:
  - aapke theek kiye hue cues       -> waise ke waise
  - aapki criticality               -> waisi ki waisi
  - khoye hue locators aur hints    -> wapas (har ek check hokar)

Poori list kya aaya kya nahi: output\pack-merge-report.json
